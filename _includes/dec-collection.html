Institutional Use Case Collection

Institutions are experimenting with a wide range of AI pilots and use cases, but many struggle to identify which are worth prioritising and scaling. To support these decisions, the DEC 2026 University Leadership Working Group has curated an evidence-based collection of AI use cases across teaching and assessment, operations, AI literacy, and curriculum development to help institutions evaluate and scale AI adoption.
