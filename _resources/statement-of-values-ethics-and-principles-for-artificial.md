Sets out 16 principles under three lenses (human-centered AI, trustworthy AI, and environmental sustainability), with guidance on resolving conflicts between principles and matching governance to risk. Written by UNM’s newly chartered AI steering committee in 2026, it shows one way to set shared principles early in AI adoption.
