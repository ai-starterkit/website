---
title: "Design an assessment that shows real learning"
kind: prompt
part: Assessment
order: 1
description: "Create · A task, a way to judge it and the conditions, built around what you want to conclude."
prompt: |-
  Objective: [objective].
  Students: [who they are].
  Purpose: [practice with feedback / check understanding mid-unit / final grade].
  Conditions: [time, tools, whether notes or AI are allowed].

  Design an assessment for this objective:
  1. State what you want to be able to conclude about a student who does well.
  2. Describe the task: what students see, do and hand in. Make sure success requires the capability itself, not a shortcut (recognizing familiar wording, guessing from answer length, pasting an AI answer when independent work is the goal).
  3. Explain how responses will be judged: an answer key, criteria or rubric that rewards what matters and accepts other valid answers.
  4. Note barriers unrelated to the objective (reading load, tools, format) and how to reduce them without changing what's being measured.
  5. If it's for practice, write the feedback a student would get, so they know what to do next.

  Choose whatever format fits the objective; don't default to multiple choice or an essay. If one task can't cover the whole objective, say what else would be needed.
---
