---
title: "Plan how students will learn it"
kind: prompt
part: Instruction
order: 1
description: "Create · A short sequence: prerequisites, explanation, practice with feedback, and support that fades."
prompt: |-
  Objective: [objective].
  Students: [who they are and what they already know].
  Format: [online page, live class, lab…], about [time].

  Plan a short learning sequence for this objective:
  1. What students need to know first, and how to check or refresh it.
  2. How to explain the key idea or procedure, making the relationships and steps that matter explicit, with an example or worked example where it helps.
  3. Where students practice the thinking or performance itself (not just reading or clicking), with guidance or feedback.
  4. The order of steps, and where support can fade as students become more capable.

  Explain briefly why each choice fits this objective and these students. Don't add media, quizzes or activities just to have them. Flag any content claim a subject expert should check.
---
