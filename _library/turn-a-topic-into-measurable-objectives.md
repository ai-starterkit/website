---
title: Turn a topic into measurable objectives
kind: prompt
part: Outcomes
order: 1
description: Gets two or three objectives that name something students do.
prompt: |-
  I teach [course] to [students]. My unit is on [topic].
  Write 2–3 learning objectives for it. Each must start with an observable verb (not “understand” or “know”), name one skill, and be something I could assess in one task. For each, tell me what evidence would show a student met it.
---
