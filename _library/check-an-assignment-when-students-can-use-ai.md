---
title: "Check an assignment when students can use AI"
kind: prompt
part: Assessment
order: 3
description: "Review · Shows what AI can fake in your assignment and how to make students' thinking visible."
prompt: |-
  Assignment: [paste].
  Objective: [objective].
  AI policy for this assignment: [not allowed / allowed for some parts / fully allowed].

  1. Complete the assignment as a student using AI would. Tell me which parts of the objective your answer would appear to show without the student having learned it.
  2. Tell me whether that's a problem, given my policy and what I want to conclude. AI use isn't automatically a problem if using it well is part of the goal.
  3. Suggest two revisions that keep the objective and make students' own thinking visible (for example, a decision with a justification, a record of their process, or applying it to their own context). Explain what each would show.
  4. Note what each revision costs students and me in time, and any access concerns.
