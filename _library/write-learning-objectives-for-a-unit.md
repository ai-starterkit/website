---
title: "Write learning objectives for a unit"
kind: prompt
part: Outcomes
order: 1
description: "Create · Clear objectives, what would show each one, and a check of the whole set."
prompt: |-
  I teach [course] to [who your students are and what they already know]. This unit is about [topic]. By the end, students should be ready to [what comes next: a later course, a job task, a project].

  Draft 3–5 learning objectives for the unit. For each one:
  1. State what students will be able to do or understand, specific enough that two instructors would picture the same result. Avoid statements that only name a topic ("learn about…") or an activity.
  2. Describe what a student would do or produce that would convince you they've achieved it, and what a weak or misleading answer would look like.
  3. Note any conditions that matter (for example: with or without notes or AI, on new cases or familiar ones).
  4. Say in one line why it matters for these students.

  Then check the set as a whole: is any important knowledge, skill or way of thinking missing, or is anything too fine-grained?

  Don't force a particular verb or sentence formula, and don't lower or change what I'm aiming for. If something I haven't told you would change your advice, ask me first. Present the result as a table.
