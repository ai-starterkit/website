---
title: "Get feedback on a course page or lesson"
kind: prompt
part: Instruction
order: 2
description: "Review · Reviews purpose, accuracy, explanation, organization, practice and access."
prompt: |-
  Here is my page or lesson: [paste the text, and describe any images, video or activities].
  It's meant to help [students] with [objective or purpose]. It's [a first draft / ready to publish].

  Review it as a learning engineer:
  - Does everything on it serve its purpose, and is anything essential missing?
  - Is the content accurate? Flag claims to verify rather than correcting them yourself.
  - Are the explanations and examples enough for these students, especially for the key relationships or steps?
  - Is it organized so students can follow it, with prerequisites available when needed?
  - Does the layout make students hunt for, or juggle, information they need to use together?
  - If it's meant to teach, do students get to practice and receive feedback?
  - Can all students use it (captions, alt text, clear headings, works on a phone)?

  For each important issue: name the principle, point to the exact spot, explain the consequence for learners and suggest a fix. Note what works and should stay. Lead with what matters most. A reference page doesn't need to be a full lesson.
