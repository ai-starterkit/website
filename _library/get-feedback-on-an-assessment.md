---
title: "Get feedback on an assessment"
kind: prompt
part: Assessment
order: 2
description: "Review · Checks whether your task, key and conditions support the conclusion you want to draw."
prompt: |-
  Here is my assessment: [paste the task or questions, and the answer key or rubric].
  It's meant to show: [objective].
  Students: [who they are]. Purpose: [practice / quiz / final]. Conditions: [time, tools, AI allowed or not].

  Review it as a learning engineer. Check:
  - Does a good response really show the objective, or could students succeed another way (cues, memorized wording, AI)?
  - Do the tasks cover enough of the objective for the conclusion I want to draw?
  - Is the key, criteria or rubric clear, correct and fair to other valid answers?
  - Could anything unrelated to the objective (language, format, access) stop a capable student from showing it?
  - If it's for learning, does the feedback tell students what to do next?
  - Do the stated conditions match what I'm claiming students can do?

  For each important issue: name the principle, point to the exact part, explain the consequence and suggest a fix that keeps the objective. Note what works and should stay. Lead with what matters most, and if missing context would change your judgment, ask instead of guessing.
