---
title: "Explain a hard idea, then practice it"
kind: prompt
part: Instruction
order: 3
description: "Create · Explanation, worked example, guided and independent practice, and feedback on likely mistakes."
prompt: |-
  Idea or procedure: [topic].
  Objective: [objective].
  Students: [what they already know, and where they usually get stuck].

  1. Write an explanation that connects the idea to what students already know and makes the key relationship or steps explicit.
  2. Give one worked example that shows each step and why it's taken.
  3. Give a similar problem with the last steps left for the student, then one new problem to do alone.
  4. Write feedback for the two most likely mistakes, explaining the thinking behind each.

  Keep it to what the objective needs. Mark anything a subject expert should check.
---
