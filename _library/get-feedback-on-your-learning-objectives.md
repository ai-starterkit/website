---
title: "Get feedback on your learning objectives"
kind: prompt
part: Outcomes
order: 2
description: "Review · An expert-style review that keeps your meaning and puts the important issues first."
prompt: |-
  Here are my learning objectives: [paste].
  Context: [course, students and what they already know, where this unit sits, how far along the design is].

  Review them as an experienced learning engineer would. Look for:
  - an intended result that is unclear, or only names a topic or activity
  - objectives where it's hard to tell what would count as evidence a student achieved them
  - a target that doesn't fit these students' starting point or the time available
  - gaps or overlaps across the set
  - wording that requires something that isn't really the point (e.g., "write an essay" when the goal is the reasoning)

  For each important issue: name the principle, point to the exact wording, explain why it matters for learning, and suggest a revision that keeps my intended meaning. Also tell me what already works and should stay.

  Put the most consequential issues first and skip minor wording preferences. Mark any rewrite that depends on assumptions about my subject. If missing context would change your judgment, tell me what you need instead of guessing.
---
