  - type: text
    id: why-it-exists
    heading: Why it exists
    body: Broad-access institutions are under increasing pressure to improve student outcomes, strengthen workforce alignment, and operate efficiently with limited resources, and AI is beginning to reshape how they work. But advancing AI adoption isn’t primarily a tooling challenge. It’s a capacity and infrastructure challenge. The AI Starter Kit builds the connective layer that helps institutions learn from one another, reduce duplication, and take a practical next step.
  - type: cards
    id: how-we-work
    style: criteria
    heading: How we work
    items:
      - title: Built with institutions
        text: People from HBCUs, community colleges and universities shape each tool and help test it.
      - title: Curated what’s working
        text: For each area, we point to a few vetted resources, with a note on what each is best for.
      - title: Build only what's missing
        text: If a tool already exists, we don’t recreate it. We only build where there's a real gap.
      - title: Open to adapt
        text: Everything we build is openly licensed as a public good, so your campus can adapt it to fit.
  - type: cards
    id: how-it-started
    style: timeline
    heading: How it started
    items:
      - title: Summer 2025
        text: "The idea starts at UNITE in Atlanta: an \"AI in a Box\" to help colleges figure out how AI can work for them."
      - title: Fall 2025
        text: Conversations with institutions, partner organizations, and funders build support for the idea and lead to the first grants.
      - title: Spring 2026
        text: Partner organizations come together to plan the AI Starter Kit.
      - title: July 2026
        text: The AI Starter Kit officially kicks off with a workshop at North Carolina Central University in Durham,  where partners mapped priorities for the work ahead.
      - title: Fall 2026
        text: Working groups take on maturity assessment, policy, technology architecture, and teaching and learning innovation. The first version of this site goes live.
      - title: Next
        text: More tools, use-cases, and resources, shaped by what broad-access institutions need most. If something would help your institution, [we'd love to hear about it](contact).
