    heading: What advising covers
    body: |-
      Academic advising is a partnership between a student and an advisor, often a faculty member or professional staff, that helps students plan their path through college.

      A bot could help with parts of this:
    points:
      - Choosing courses and registering each term
      - Planning degree, general education and minor requirements
      - Pointing students to tutoring, counseling, disability services and financial aid
      - Explaining deadlines, credit transfer and other rules
    source: Based on [NACADA's definitions of academic advising](https://nacada.ksu.edu/Resources/Clearinghouse/View-Articles/Definitions-of-academic-advising) and the advising guides of several universities.
