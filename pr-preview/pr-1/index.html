What is it?

AI adoption across broad-access institutions is early and fragmented, and many experiments lack the infrastructure, governance, or capacity to scale. Built by broad-access institutions, for broad-access institutions, the AI Starter Kit is shifting that work toward a collaborative model, where a community of practice develops reusable tools and shared infrastructure.
