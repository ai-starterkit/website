Interdisciplinary Transformation University (ITU Austria)

Apollo.AI: AI Boardroom Tool for Leadership Decisions

  • Stage: 3 Piloting & Capability Building
  • Scope: Operational Readiness
  • Scale: Single-tool pilot

What it is

ITU Austria ran a proof-of-concept pilot of Apollo.AI, a boardroom software tool, with the university's Leadership Board. The tool was designed to support meeting preparation, confidential document storage and sharing with watermarking, hybrid and online decision-making facilitation, note-taking and summaries, and an audit-proof decision log.

Problem addressed

Leadership board meetings lacked streamlined tooling for preparation, documentation, and decision traceability. Finding a Europe-based, preferably regional, solution was a driving external factor alongside leadership support as an internal one.

How it was built

A need was identified and a client brief was submitted to the university's Change Forum, which consisted of five voting members (the university's four directors and managing director) and four advisory members (Head of HR, Head of Legal, one professor representative, and the strategy manager). The Change Forum approved the pilot. Apollo.AI had already been identified as a promising candidate. No system integration was required during the pilot phase, and the tool was set up by one person in approximately two hours.

Cost & resources

There were no extra costs during the pilot phase. Setup required one person and approximately two hours.

Outcomes

The pilot was still ongoing at the time of submission, with an end date of March 26, 2026 and an institutional decision required by June 30, 2026. Early results were described as positive. Anticipated benefits included reduced manual effort and errors, streamlined procedures across boards, improved efficiency of major decision-making bodies, and improved transparency and traceability of decisions.

Risks

Identified risks included requirement misalignment across different boards that may have different processes and existing solutions; integration complexity with MS Teams, SharePoint, Outlook Calendar, and signing software (Sproof); a tight timeline to June 30, 2026; and data protection concerns for sensitive documents requiring correct role-based permissions.
