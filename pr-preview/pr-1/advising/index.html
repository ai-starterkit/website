AI Advising

Many advising questions come up again and again. We're exploring a bot that could take on some of the routine ones, so advisors have more time for the conversations that need a person.

Work in progress

This page is a first sketch. It will grow as the work takes shape.

What advising covers

Academic advising is a partnership between a student and an advisor, often a faculty member or professional staff, that helps students plan their path through college.

A bot could help with parts of this:

  • Choosing courses and registering each term
  • Planning degree, general education and minor requirements
  • Pointing students to tutoring, counseling, disability services and financial aid
  • Explaining deadlines, credit transfer and other rules

Based on NACADA's definitions of academic advising and the advising guides of several universities.

Where a bot could help

How the tool works is still being shaped. The idea is to share the load, with advisors in charge.

A bot could help with

  • Deadlines and registration steps
  • Where to find tutoring, counseling and other campus services
  • Reading program requirements or a degree audit
  • General policies, such as adding or dropping a class

Advisors stay essential for

  • Career and life goals
  • Academic probation and other difficult situations
  • Plans that depend on a student's circumstances
  • Supporting students as they make their own final decisions
Open to everyone
