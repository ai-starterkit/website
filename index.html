AI Starter Kit

From Innovative Experiments to Shared Resources and Infrastructure

AI adoption across broad-access colleges and universities is early and fragmented. Many experiments lack the infrastructure, governance, or capacity to scale. The AI Starter Kit is an effort by higher education institutions for higher education institutions to shift from isolated experimentations to a collaborative model, where reusable tools and shared infrastructure are developed by a community of practice.

What's in the Kit

  • AI Policy
  • Technology Architecture
  • Maturity Assessments
  • Teaching & Learning Innovation
  • Advising and Student Supports
  • Capacity Building

Who It's For

Institutional leaders, Chief AI Officers, IT staff, intermediary organizations that are working with colleges and universities, as well as interested students, staff, and faculty.
